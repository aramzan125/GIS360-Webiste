Request checklist

Before Submitting a Support Request

To help GIS360 coordinate your request efficiently, provide as much relevant information as possible:

  • Your name and company
  • Email and telephone number
  • Site/location
  • Description of the issue or task
  • Affected device/system where applicable
  • Business impact and error message
  • Preferred contact method
  • Required attendance date where applicable
  • Ticket, customer or site reference
  • Asset information where available

Not every item is mandatory. Existing customers should include relevant references where available.

Priority Guidance

Describe the actual impact

Critical

A major business-critical service or location is unavailable and there is significant operational impact.

High

A serious issue is substantially affecting users or operations but some business functionality remains available.

Normal

A standard support request, individual-user issue, planned task or non-critical technical requirement.

Priority classification and response targets may be adjusted in accordance with the customer's service agreement and the actual impact of the incident.
