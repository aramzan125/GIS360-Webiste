4. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, GIS360 may rely on one or more lawful bases under applicable data-protection legislation.

Contract and Pre-Contractual Measures

Processing may be necessary to provide contracted services, administer customer relationships, respond to requests before entering into a contract, or process applications and other requests initiated by an individual.

Legitimate Interests

GIS360 may process information where necessary to operate and secure our website, manage business communications, prevent fraud or misuse, maintain service quality, handle support requests and protect our systems and business operations. We consider the impact on individuals before relying on legitimate interests.

Legal Obligations

Information may be processed where required to comply with obligations imposed by applicable law.

Consent

Where processing genuinely requires consent, such as certain non-essential cookies or optional communications, consent will be requested separately. Consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

6. Who May Receive Personal Data

Personal data may be accessible only where necessary to:

  • authorised GIS360 personnel;
  • technical personnel involved in delivering requested services;
  • hosting and infrastructure providers;
  • email and communication service providers;
  • professional advisers where required;
  • public authorities where disclosure is legally required.

Service providers processing personal data on our behalf must be subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations. GIS360 does not sell personal data.

7. International Data Transfers

Some service providers or technical systems may process data outside Portugal or the European Economic Area.

Where an international transfer is subject to GDPR requirements, GIS360 will use an appropriate transfer mechanism where required, such as an adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses or another legally recognised safeguard.

8. Data Retention

GIS360 retains personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and as required by applicable legal obligations.

Retention may depend on the nature of the relationship, contractual requirements, legal obligations, the duration of an enquiry or support case, security requirements and potential legal claims. Applicant and recruitment information is subject to the retention arrangements described in the Applicant Privacy Notice. Personal data should not be kept indefinitely without an identifiable purpose.

9. Data Security

GIS360 applies appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data from unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration, improper disclosure, destruction and misuse.

Access to personal information is restricted according to operational requirements. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security, but GIS360 takes reasonable measures to reduce security risks.

10. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable legal conditions, individuals may have rights including:

  • Access — request confirmation and access to personal data.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure — request deletion where legal requirements are satisfied.
  • Restriction — request limitation of processing in applicable circumstances.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests where applicable.
  • Portability — request portable data where the legal requirements apply.
  • Withdrawal of Consent — withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests can be submitted to info@gis360.eu. GIS360 may request reasonable information necessary to verify identity before responding to a data-rights request.
