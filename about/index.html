About GIS360

Global IT Expertise. Delivered Where Business Happens.

GIS360 is a technology services company providing organisations with dependable IT support, field engineering, managed services and specialist technical expertise across multiple locations and regions.

We help businesses maintain, support and extend their technology environments through a coordinated service model that combines remote support, on-site engineering and specialised technical resources.

From a single technical intervention to ongoing multi-site support, our focus remains the same: reliable delivery, clear communication and consistent technical standards.

Our Approach

From Request to Resolution

GIS360 is built around structured service delivery. We begin by understanding the requirement, location, technical scope and expected outcome. From there, we coordinate the appropriate support team, field engineer or specialist resource and manage the request through delivery and completion.

01

Understand

Clarify the requirement, location, technical scope and expected outcome.

02

Coordinate

Select the appropriate support team, engineer or specialist resource.

03

Deploy

Assign the right technical capability according to the requirement.

04

Deliver

Manage the work through execution and completion.

05

Support

Maintain clear communication and provide continued support where required.

Whether the requirement involves a remote support incident, an engineer dispatch, a network task, an ERP requirement or additional technical resources, GIS360 focuses on making the process clear and dependable.

Global Coordination

Local Expertise. Global Coordination.

Technology requirements do not stop at geographical boundaries.

GIS360 supports organisations that operate across multiple offices, customer sites and international locations by combining central coordination with access to technical resources where work needs to be performed.

This model allows customers to maintain consistent service standards while reducing the complexity of coordinating different providers across different locations.

From remote troubleshooting to on-site technical intervention, our objective is to make international IT delivery feel organised, responsive and manageable.
