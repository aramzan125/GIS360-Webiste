Proactive Management of Your IT Environment

Managed IT Services

GIS360 Managed IT Services help businesses move from reactive troubleshooting toward structured, proactive IT management.

We support the day-to-day operation of business technology environments through monitoring, maintenance, technical support, security management and performance optimisation.

Our objective is straightforward: identify and address technical issues before they develop into larger operational problems while providing organisations with dependable technical support when intervention is required.

Managed IT Capabilities

  • Proactive IT monitoring
  • Preventive maintenance
  • Endpoint and infrastructure support
  • Managed antivirus and security services
  • Software and patch-management assistance
  • System performance optimisation
  • Incident coordination
  • Technical troubleshooting
  • Infrastructure health monitoring
  • Vendor coordination
  • Service reporting
  • Ongoing technical support

Proactive Rather Than Reactive

Traditional IT support often begins after something has already failed.

GIS360’s managed-services approach focuses on continually maintaining the technology environment so potential issues can be identified earlier and routine maintenance remains controlled.

Flexible Service Delivery

Managed IT requirements differ between organisations.

GIS360 can support existing internal IT departments or provide broader outsourced technical capabilities according to the customer’s operational requirements.
